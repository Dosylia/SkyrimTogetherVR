# The victim in the PvP damage test. Sits still, gets hit, and deliberately says nothing afterwards.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair pvp
#
# Sending its own health after being hit would overwrite the server's stored value with a correct one, which is
# the value the test exists to inspect. Standing still is the whole job.

log pvp victim
waitfor connected 45
collect health death spawn
record pvp victim

waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me

health 100
wait 4
expect health me == 100

log standing still while the other player hits me
wait 50
expect connected

record off
report bot-pvp-actor.txt
log done
stop
