# The copy's hands are where the owner's are.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-hands
#
# Measured on 2026-10-03, both screens: a copy's hands sat a median 27-32 units (about 40 cm) from where their owner
# held them, mostly too far forward -- hands that touched in life were most of a metre apart on screen, and so was
# whatever they held or dragged. The pose is joint rotations, and the same rotations on another body put the hands
# elsewhere. Since 2026-10-04 the owner's hand positions travel with every pose and the copy's arms are bent to put
# the hands there (VRBodySync::ReachHands).
#
# The bot records the game player's own stream (movement and VR pose) and plays it back as its own character, with
# the hands put at the waist (the rig's headset has no controllers, so the recording has them on the floor, beyond
# any arm's reach); the game poses the bot's copy and logs, every 5 s, where the hands should be against where they are.
log live hands
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

# A copy outside the headset's view is not posed at all (and not measured), and the rig's headset does not turn with
# the player ("out of view" in every frame, 2026-10-04). Anything within 100 units of the headset counts as in view,
# so the player is put 50 units from the copy.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 copy f:50 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 3
log -- recording the player's stream for 12 s
capture other 12
wait 1
log -- this bot plays it back as its own character, hands at the waist the way real logs have them
handtargets -15 15 70 15 20 80
replay me 3
wait 3
log CHECK hands within 6
log SHOT hands of copy
wait 4
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
