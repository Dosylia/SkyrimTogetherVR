# The defender's rule (Physics queue P4): a hit from the other player is dropped when this game saw that player's
# weapon meet the player's own around the moment the hit is seen here.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-defender
#
# The steps of live-clash give a clash in the player's game: the HIGGS-held sword meets the copy's sword, and the clash
# reaches this bot with the server tick it was seen at. The bot hits the player twice for 30: first before any clash,
# which should land, then stamped 225 ms before the clash (the VR playback delay: the hit is seen on the player's
# screen just as the blades met), which the player's game should drop.
log live defender
waitfor connected 45
# Daylight, for a screenshot that shows something (the test game's clock; her saves are put back afterwards).
log DO console set gamehour to 13
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

# The copy draws (see live-sword-body).
additem 12EB7
equip 12EB7
wait 1
draw on
wait 1
teleport away
wait 6
teleport back
wait 8
log CHECK logged Player copy \w+ came with its weapon drawn

# In view of the headset, then the copy posed by the player's own stream, held after the replay.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 copy f:50 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 3
capture other 12
wait 1
handtargets -15 15 70 15 20 80
holdpose on
replay me 2
wait 3
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon .* is a body in the world now

# A sword in the player's right hand, by HIGGS. The bot waits out each step: live-check performs them on the game's side
# and they take seconds, and the copy (with its sword) is gone as soon as the bot stops.
log DO papyrus Actor SheatheWeapon 14
wait 4
log DO drop 12EB7 here
wait 7
log DO grab dropped right
wait 5
log DO sample held 2
wait 4

# A hit before anything of the player's has touched the copy's weapons: lands. (After, a hand left resting on a blade
# blocks every hit, as the rule says: 20:33, "blocked ... 510 ms before it was seen here".)
log CHECK player mark
wait 1
hit other -30
wait 2
log CHECK player dropped 30
log CHECK logged Defender's rule: a hit of 30 from player \d+ lands

# Onto the copy's blade.
log DO bring held to copy weapon
wait 3
log DO sample held 4
wait 6
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ (left|right) weapon touches a body .*(held by the player's HIGGS hand|one of the player's HIGGS bodies)
log CHECK logged Clash: our (left|right) hand .*met the (left|right) weapon of \w+ .*felt and heard here, sent
waitfor clashes >= 1 10
log SHOT held against copy of copy
log CHECK game alive
wait 4

# A hit seen on the player's screen just as the blades met: blocked.
log CHECK player mark
wait 1
hit other -30 atclash
wait 2
log CHECK player dropped 0
log CHECK logged Defender's rule: a hit of 30 from player \d+ is blocked

# The window's early edge: a clash counts from 325 ms before the hit happened (300 ms before it, and it is seen 225 ms
# after). Stamped 300 ms before the first clash a hit is blocked; 360 ms before it, it lands -- nothing of the player's
# had touched this bot's weapons yet.
log CHECK player mark
wait 1
hit other -30 atclash -300
wait 2
log CHECK player dropped 0
log CHECK player mark
wait 1
hit other -30 atclash -360
wait 2
log CHECK player dropped 30

# The other player's delay alone, every 10 s.
log CHECK logged InterpDiag players: \d+ updates

# One meeting, a clash or two -- not one every quarter second while the hand rests on the blade.
expect clashes <= 4

# The other way: this bot's hand met the player's right weapon.
clash other right
wait 3
log CHECK logged Clash from server \d+: their right hand met our right weapon
log CHECK logged Clash from server \d+: heard, felt
log CHECK logged Clash: sparks at \(

holdpose off
wait 2
log done
stop
