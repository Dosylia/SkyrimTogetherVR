# The listening half of the voice test; the actor's script says what it does and why.
#
# "party lead": this bot leads the party, so the actor joins it as a member on arriving (the server's automatic
# party) and the party channel can be tested before it leaves.

log voice watcher
party lead
waitfor connected 45
record voice watcher
voice on

waitfor known other 40

log near, in the party
waitfor voices >= 38 40
log far, in the party
waitfor voices >= 78 60
log back near, out of the party: the far phase between must have added nothing
waitfor voices >= 118 90
wait 6
expect voices <= 122

log the server says where the other player is, every two seconds
expect whereabouts >= 10

record off
report bot-voice-watcher.txt
log done
stop
