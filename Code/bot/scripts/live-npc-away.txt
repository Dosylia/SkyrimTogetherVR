# An NPC that dies while the player is somewhere else: is it dead when the player comes back?
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-npc-away
#
# VR_TODO, "a copy that goes out of range is never put right when you come back": a body left where it was last
# seen is the oldest report there is, and "the stale-copy repair now applies death state" has been waiting for a
# session since 2026-09-26. The player is moved by console (a "DO console" line, run by the driver): out through a
# load door to wherever Lydia is, and back to one of Mistwatch's own bandits. This bot is not standalone, so it
# follows the player's cell both ways; the bear stays where it was registered and stays this bot's.
log live npc away
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn health death ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- bringing a bear
npc temp 23A8A
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc alive
wait 3

# Where Lydia stands is under water in Emma's save; without this the player drowns in half a minute.
log DO god on
wait 3
log -- the player leaves through a load door
log DO console player.moveto A2C94
wait 40

log -- the bear dies while the player is away
npclife dead 0
wait 6

log -- the player comes back
log DO console player.moveto 45A63
wait 45
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc dead
wait 3

log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
