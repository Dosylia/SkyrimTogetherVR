# The caster in the spell damage test: burns the other bot as a concentration spell does, then reconnects to be
# given a fresh copy of it built from what the server stored.
log spell caster
waitfor connected 45
collect health death spawn
record spell caster
waitfor known other 40
waitfor health other >= 100 30
expect health other >= 100
# The victim sets its own health once on arrival; burning before that lands would be overwritten (see pvp).
log letting the other player settle
wait 10

log burning the other player: twelve changes of -2 over three seconds
burn other 8 3
wait 6

log reconnecting, to be handed a fresh copy built from what the server stored
disconnect
wait 3
reconnect
waitfor connected 45
waitfor known other 40
log all twelve must be in the stored health
expect health other >= 75.5
expect health other <= 76.5
expect alive other
record off
report bot-spell-watcher.txt
log done
stop
