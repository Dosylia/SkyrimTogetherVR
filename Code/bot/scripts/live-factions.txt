# Factions corrected on return: a creature that changed sides while the player was away comes back on its new side.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-factions
#
# Faction changes travel to players in range only, so a player who was away missed them; the spawn re-sent on return
# carries them (CharacterService, "Factions, for the same reason and with more at stake than they look" -- the Burned
# Spriggan of 2026-09-25 that squared up to Lydia and never swung). Here the bot's bear is put in the player's
# faction (DB1) while the player is away.
log live factions
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
stay

log -- a bear of this bot's
npc temp 23A8A
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc notinfaction DB1
wait 3

log DO god on
wait 3
log -- the player leaves
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 8
wait 40

log -- while the player is away, the bear joins the player's faction
faction npc DB1 0
wait 5

log -- the player comes back
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 -9
wait 40
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc infaction DB1
wait 3

log -- and a change while the player is here arrives too
faction npc 1BCC0 0
wait 6
log CHECK npc infaction 1BCC0
log CHECK npc notinfaction DB1
wait 3
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
