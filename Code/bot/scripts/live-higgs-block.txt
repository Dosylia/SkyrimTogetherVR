# A sword held with HIGGS against the other player's physical sword (Physics queue P3).
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-higgs-block
#
# The copy holds a drawn iron sword, posed as in live-sword-body, so its sword is a body in this world (VRWeaponBody).
# The player's right HIGGS hand grabs an iron sword from the ground by script (HiggsVR.GrabObject; the rig has no
# controllers to grab with), and the player is moved so that sword meets the centre of the copy's blade. A held object
# is dynamic, pulled to the hand by HIGGS's grab constraint; the copy's sword is keyframed. Havok should push the held
# sword out of the blade: it stops there instead of passing through. The sampled positions say how far it stays from
# the blade and how far from the player's hand, and the client logs what the copy's sword touches.
log live higgs block
waitfor connected 45
# Daylight, for a screenshot that shows something (the test game's clock; her saves are put back afterwards).
log DO console set gamehour to 13
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

# The copy draws (see live-sword-body).
additem 12EB7
equip 12EB7
wait 1
draw on
wait 1
teleport away
wait 6
teleport back
wait 8
log CHECK logged Player copy \w+ came with its weapon drawn

# In view of the headset, then the copy posed by the player's own stream, held after the replay.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 copy f:50 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 3
capture other 12
wait 1
handtargets -15 15 70 15 20 80
holdpose on
replay me 2
wait 3
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon .* is a body in the world now

# A sword in the player's right hand, by HIGGS. The bot waits out each step: live-check performs them on the game's side
# and they take seconds, and the copy (with its sword) is gone as soon as the bot stops.
log DO papyrus Actor SheatheWeapon 14
wait 4
log DO drop 12EB7 here
wait 7
log DO grab dropped right
wait 5
log DO sample held 2
wait 4

# Onto the copy's blade.
log DO bring held to copy weapon
wait 3
log DO sample held 4
wait 6
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon touches a body
log SHOT held against copy of copy
log CHECK game alive
wait 4

holdpose off
wait 2
log done
stop
