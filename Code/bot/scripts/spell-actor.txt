# The victim in the spell damage test. Sits still while the other bot burns it, and checks that every bit of it
# arrived.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-all-pairs.ps1 -Pairs spell
#
# The pvp pair sends one sword-sized hit. A concentration spell is different on the wire: flames take a fraction of a
# point a frame, and the client adds those up and sends them every quarter second (ActorValueService::
# RunSmallHealthUpdates). "I damage him, he takes nothing" was the report; this is the half of it a bot can check --
# that a stream of small changes reaches the target whole. Whether the game raises them for a spell on a remote copy
# at all needs a headset.
log spell victim
waitfor connected 45
collect health death spawn
record spell victim
waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me
health 100
wait 4
expect health me == 100

log standing still while the other player burns me: 8 a second for 3 seconds
waitfor health me <= 76.5 40
wait 2
expect health me >= 75.5
expect health me <= 76.5
expect alive me

# Saying nothing afterwards: sending its own health would overwrite what the server stored, which the other side
# is about to read.
log holding still while the other side checks what the server kept
wait 25
expect connected
record off
report bot-spell-actor.txt
log done
stop
