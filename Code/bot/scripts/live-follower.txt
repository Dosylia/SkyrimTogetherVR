#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-follower

# 2026-10-03 15:09 and 2026-10-04 09:33: Emma went through a door, the server gave Lydia (still inside, near Seen)
# to Seen, and Lydia followed Emma out anyway. The two games then pulled her back and forth -- 85 appearances in 12 s
# -- and Seen's game dressed her in the outfit she has in his own save, which Emma's game applied. Now the player's
# game keeps its follower's gear and asks for her back at once.

# Here: Lydia (A2C94, Emma's follower in her save) is put next to the player; the player travels away while this bot
# stays and accepts what is left, so the server hands Lydia to the bot; then she is brought back next to the player,
# the way a follower catches up.
