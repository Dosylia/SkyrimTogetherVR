# Do the creatures the mod had no animation data for still slide on the other screen?
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-creatures
#
# Lurker, Netch and Seeker have their own replacer folders since 2026-09-30/10-01, and the game picks them up; what
# was left open is whether a copy of one on the *other* player's screen then moves its legs, which needed two real
# games. One game and this bot will do: the game spawns the creature and lets it attack the player (combat AI on,
# god mode on), this bot records the stream the server relays for it, registers a creature of the same kind itself,
# and plays the stream back. The game's copy of that creature is then asked whether its animation graph has a speed
# while it travels ("gait").
log live creatures
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 6
log DO god on
wait 2
log DO combat on
wait 2

log -- seeker: the game spawns one, and it comes for the player
log DO console player.placeatme 0201EEC9 1
wait 6
capture base 1EEC9 12
log -- a creature of the same kind, brought by this bot, plays that back
npc captured
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 6
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc gait walking 9
replay npc 2
log SHOT seeker of npc
wait 4

log -- lurker: the game spawns one, and it comes for the player
log DO console player.placeatme 02014494 1
wait 6
capture base 14494 12
log -- a creature of the same kind, brought by this bot, plays that back
npc captured
waitfor owned == 2 20
wait 6
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc gait walking 9
replay npc 2
log SHOT lurker of npc
wait 4

log -- netch: the game spawns one, and it comes for the player
log DO console player.placeatme 0202173B 1
wait 6
capture base 2173B 12
log -- a creature of the same kind, brought by this bot, plays that back
npc captured
waitfor owned == 3 20
wait 6
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc gait walking 9
replay npc 2
log SHOT netch of npc
wait 4

log DO combat off
wait 2
log DO god off
wait 2
log done
stop
