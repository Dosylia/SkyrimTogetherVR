# Walks several cells away and comes back, the way a player crossing Solstheim does.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair range
#
# Seen crossed seven grid squares in three minutes on 2026-09-25, and the bandit through the sky, the body in the
# wrong place and the spriggan that could not land a hit all happened during it. A grid square is 4096 units, so
# the trip below is about five of them each way -- far enough for the server to take this bot out of range of the
# other one and put it back.

log range actor
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership
record range actor

waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me

log settling where the other player can see me
wait 8

log walking five cells east
walk rel 20000 0 3000
wait 10

# Changing health out here is the point. Health travels in range only, so the watcher cannot be told about this
# one: the only way it can ever learn the new value is the spawn the server re-sends when this bot comes back.
log taking damage where the other side cannot be told about it
health 55
wait 6

log and back again
walk rel -20000 0 3000
wait 10

expect known me
expect connected

log standing still while the other side counts what it has
wait 10
expect connected

record off
report bot-range-actor.txt
log done
stop
