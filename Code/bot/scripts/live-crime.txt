# Does the crime alarm guard fire, and does the game survive a crime while connected?
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-crime
#
# The guard on the crime alarm (Actor::StealAlarm, hooked since 2026-09-21 after a fault in it ended four sessions)
# had never run: nobody committed a crime in a session since. Here the player "steals" a horse at Windhelm's docks,
# where there are people to see it, through the game's own SendStealAlarm. The hook logs its first three runs
# ("Crime alarm ran"). The bounty is real in this session and gone with it: nothing is saved.
log live crime
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5
stay

log DO god on
wait 3
log -- to Windhelm's docks, where there are people to see a crime
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 8
wait 40
log CHECK game alive

log -- the player steals a horse, as far as the witnesses are concerned
log DO papyrus ObjectReference SendStealAlarm 9848C 14
wait 6
log CHECK logged Crime alarm ran
log CHECK game alive
wait 3

log -- back
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 -9
wait 40
log CHECK game alive
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
