# Death, respawn and recovery, three rounds back to back.
#
# The second and third deaths are the point: a single death and respawn always looked fine, and things came
# apart when they happened in a row. Each round asserts the exact health at every step, so a regression in the
# damage sign, the respawn value or the death state fails here rather than in a play session.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Script death-recovery.txt

log Death and recovery, three rounds
waitfor connected 45
collect health death ownership
record death recovery

expect players >= 1
expect known me

log round 1
health 100
wait 2
expect health me == 100
damage 40
wait 2
expect health me == 60
die
wait 6
expect health me <= 0
respawn
wait 6
expect health me > 0
expect connected

log round 2, straight after
health 100
wait 2
expect health me == 100
damage 40
wait 2
expect health me == 60
die
wait 6
expect health me <= 0
respawn
wait 6
expect health me > 0
expect connected

log round 3, with movement in between
walk rel 300 0 200
wait 2
health 100
wait 2
damage 40
wait 2
expect health me == 60
die
wait 6
expect health me <= 0
respawn
wait 6
walk rel -300 0 200
wait 2
expect health me > 0
expect connected
expect players >= 1
expect known me

record off
report bot-death-recovery.txt
log done
stop
