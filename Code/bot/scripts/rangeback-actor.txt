# The walker in the come-back test: walks several cells away and back, and checks that its copy of the player who
# stayed was put right on the way in.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-all-pairs.ps1 -Pairs rangeback
#
# The mirror of the range pair. There the walker changes while away and the one who stayed must see it; here the one
# who stayed changes, and the walker must see it on coming back. That is the other half of VR_TODO's "two range paths
# are asymmetric": a character that moves out of range is removed for its viewers, but a viewer that walks away is
# never told to drop what it left behind, so its copies go stale. Coming back has to repair them.
log rangeback walker
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn health
record rangeback walker
waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
waitfor health other >= 100 30
wait 4

log walking five cells east
walk rel 20000 0 3000
log giving the other player time to lose health where it cannot be sent to me
wait 10
# Out of range: the change must not have arrived, and since 2026-10-10 the server removes what a viewer walked away
# from (PlayerService, the grid shift), so the copy is gone rather than held at its old health.
expect not health other <= 60
expect not known other

log and back again
walk rel -20000 0 3000
log the copy must be put right on the way back in
waitfor health other <= 55 30
expect health other == 55
expect alive other
expect actors == 2
expect connected

log holding on so the other side finishes
wait 5
record off
report bot-rangeback-actor.txt
log done
stop
