# The speaking half of the voice test (VR_TODO, "Proximity voice chat", steps 2 and 5).
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-all-pairs.ps1 -Pairs voice -PairsAtOnce 1
#
# Four times 40 pieces of voice: near and in the party, far and in the party (the party channel, heard at any
# distance), far after leaving the party (not heard: proximity only), near again outside the party (heard). The
# watcher counts what reaches it: 120 is right, 160 means a voice reached someone too far away to hear it.
# Filler pieces ("speak test"): the server routes them without looking inside, and no game decodes them here.

log voice actor
waitfor connected 45
record voice actor
voice on

waitfor players >= 1 30
log waiting for the watcher to turn its voice on
wait 5

log 1: near, in the party
speak test 40
wait 5

log 2: five cells away, still in the party: the party channel
walk rel 20000 0 3000
wait 4
speak test 40
wait 5

log 3: far and out of the party: nobody hears this
party leave
wait 3
speak test 40
wait 5

log 4: back near, out of the party: heard by proximity
walk rel -20000 0 3000
wait 4
speak test 40
wait 8

expect connected
record off
report bot-voice-actor.txt
log done
stop
