# Hits on the other player's NPC reach its owner and come back: the player here burns the bot's bear.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-hit-npc
#
# The bear belongs to this bot, so the game holds a copy it does not run. A Firebolt cast by the player at the copy
# (Spell.Cast, there is no sword arm without a controller) has to reach this bot as damage to its bear, and what this
# bot then reports as the owner -- the bear dead -- has to come back to the copy. The copy's own health does not move
# when it is hit: the owner decides (first run, 2026-10-03: the bot saw -109, the copy stayed at 100 until told).
log live hit npc
waitfor connected 45
collect health spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- a bear of this bot's
npc temp 23A8A
waitfor owned == 1 20
wait 8
log CHECK npc exists
log CHECK npc health 100
wait 3

log -- the player casts a Firebolt at it
log DO papyrus Spell Cast 12FCD 14 npc
wait 6
expect health npc < 100
wait 3

# This bot does not simulate its bear: a real owner's game would apply the damage and report the result. It reports
# here what the damage did -- the bear dead -- and that has to come back to the game's copy.
log -- the owner reports its bear dead
npclife dead 0
wait 6
log CHECK npc dead
wait 3
log done
stop
