# The main unattended suite: two bots, no human, no headset.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1
#
# Every check here asserts a value, not just that the connection survived. Until 2026-09-25 these were all
# "players >= 1" and "connected", which pass whatever the code does; a green test that cannot fail is worse than
# no test, because it is counted as coverage.

log Automated suite: two bots, no human
waitfor connected 45
collect health death ownership spawn party
record automated suite

log 1. the other player is visible and this bot has a character
waitfor players >= 1 20
expect players >= 1
expect known me

log 2. health falls by exactly what was dealt, and does not rise
health 100
wait 2
expect health me == 100
damage 30
wait 2
expect health me == 70
damage 20
wait 2
expect health me == 50

log 3. death takes health to zero or below, respawn brings it back
die
wait 6
expect health me <= 0
respawn
wait 6
expect health me > 0
expect health me <= 100
expect connected

log 4. movement does not drop the connection or the character
walk rel 600 0 200
wait 3
walk rel -600 0 200
wait 3
expect connected
expect known me
expect players >= 1

log 5. after a reconnect the world is whole again
disconnect
wait 4
reconnect
waitfor connected 45
expect players >= 1
expect known me
health 100
wait 2
expect health me == 100

record off
report bot-auto-suite.txt
stop
