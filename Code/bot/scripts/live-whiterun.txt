# The Whiterun gate: a player who goes inside the walls is in another worldspace, so the other player's game drops
# their copy, and gets it back when they come out.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-whiterun
#
# VR_TODO, "Whiterun gate: the copy goes when inside and comes back when out" (a player "vanishing" near the gate,
# 2026-09-20). The player travels by cell: to the plains outside the gate (Tamriel 5,-3), then
# inside (WhiterunWorld), where this bot stays behind, and out again. A trip by coordinates sends no cell-change
# message, so the bot does not follow by itself: it teleports to the same square.
log live whiterun
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log DO god on
wait 3
log -- to the plains outside Whiterun's gate, and this bot with it
stay
log DO console cow Tamriel 5 -3
wait 30
teleport 22400 -10100
waitfor known other 30
log CHECK game inworld
expect known other

log -- the player goes inside the walls; this bot stays outside
log DO console cow WhiterunWorld 5 -3
wait 40
log CHECK game inworld
log CHECK logged Grid change
expect not known other

log -- and comes back out
log DO console cow Tamriel 5 -3
wait 40
log CHECK game inworld
waitfor known other 20
expect known other
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
