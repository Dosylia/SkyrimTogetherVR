# The real game as the sender: what the player does here reaches the other player.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-sender
#
# Every other live test has the bot do things and the game watch. Here it is the other way round: the player loses
# health, takes up a sword, kills a bear of this cell, dies and is brought back, and goes elsewhere -- and this bot
# checks each one arrived. "ref:860F8" is a bear placed near Mistwatch, a reference of the game's own; not the
# Mistwatch bandit 45A63, who is already dead in Emma's save (the first run "saw" him die 18 ms after the order).
# Killed through the game's own Actor.Kill: the VR console does not take "860F8.kill" ("Script command not found"),
# and "prid 860F8" then "kill" did nothing either (2026-10-03).
log live sender
waitfor connected 45
collect health death equipment spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 5

log -- the player loses health
log DO console player.damageav health 60
waitfor health other <= 290 20
expect health other <= 290

log -- the player takes up a sword
log DO console player.additem 12EB7 1
wait 2
log DO console player.equipitem 12EB7
waitfor equipped other 12EB7 20
expect equipped other 12EB7

log -- the player kills a bear of this cell, alive until now
# Brought next to the player first: bears wander, and in the test save of 2026-10-03 this one is out of the loaded
# cells, so it was never announced and the bot never heard of it.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 860F8 14 f:300 f:0 f:0 b:true
wait 5
log CHECK ref 860F8 alive
wait 3
waitfor known ref:860F8 30
expect known ref:860F8
expect alive ref:860F8
log DO papyrus Actor Kill 860F8
waitfor dead ref:860F8 20
expect dead ref:860F8
log CHECK ref 860F8 dead
wait 3

log -- the player dies and the mod brings them back
log DO console player.kill
waitfor dead other 20
expect dead other
waitfor alive other 40
expect alive other

log -- the player goes elsewhere; this bot follows the player's cell when told
log DO god on
wait 3
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 8
wait 40
expect samecell other
log DO console cow Tamriel 34 -9
wait 40
expect samecell other
log DO god off
wait 3
log done
stop
