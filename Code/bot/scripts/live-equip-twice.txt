# An equip of the weapon a copy already holds: does the copy's hand stay full? (VR_TODO, "An equip of a weapon the
# copy already holds may leave its hand empty".)
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-equip-twice
#
# On 2026-10-03 the bot's copy arrived already holding the iron sword and the bot equipped it again: the game then
# said the sword was equipped and the right hand held nothing. On 2026-10-09 Emma's copy in Seen's game held no weapon
# for two minutes amid repeated equips. Here: the same sword equipped once, again, then twice at once.
log live equip twice
waitfor connected 45
collect equipment spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 8
log CHECK copy exists

additem 12EB7 1
wait 4
log CHECK copy has 12EB7

log -- the sword in the right hand
equip 12EB7
wait 6
log CHECK copy righthand 12EB7

log -- the same sword equipped again
equip 12EB7
wait 6
log CHECK copy equipped 12EB7
log CHECK copy righthand 12EB7

log -- twice more, at once
equip 12EB7
equip 12EB7
wait 6
log CHECK copy equipped 12EB7
log CHECK copy righthand 12EB7
# The checks are asked of the game while the script goes on: without this wait the unequip below arrived before the
# last check was answered, and it read an empty hand (2026-10-10, both runs).
wait 3

unequip 12EB7
wait 4
log done
stop
