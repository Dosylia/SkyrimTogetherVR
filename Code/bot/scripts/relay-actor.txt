# The acting half of the relay test: does things to itself on a schedule the watcher waits on.
#
# Run it through the harness rather than by hand:
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Relay
#
# The respawn here is deliberately "bare": it asks the server to respawn and then says nothing more. A real client
# sends its restored health on a later tick, and if this bot did the same it would quietly repair the server's
# stored health before the watcher could look at it -- the bug of 2026-09-25 would never be visible.

log relay actor
waitfor connected 45
collect health death
record relay actor

waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me

log settle
health 100
wait 6
expect health me == 100

log taking damage the watcher should see
damage 50
wait 8

log dying
die
wait 10
expect health me <= 0

log respawning, and saying nothing afterwards
respawn bare
wait 40
expect connected

record off
report bot-relay-actor.txt
log done
stop
