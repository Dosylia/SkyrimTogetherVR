# The leading half of the dragonfly pair (see dragonfly-actor.txt). It leads the party and asks for the other side's
# dragon twice: while it flies (refused) and after it has landed (granted).
log dragonfly watcher
party lead
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership party
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
waitfor known ref:A2C94 40
wait 4
log asking for the dragon while it flies
claim ref:A2C94
wait 3
expect owned == 0
wait 6
log asking again once it has landed
claim ref:A2C94
waitfor owned == 1 10
expect owned == 1
wait 3
log done
stop
