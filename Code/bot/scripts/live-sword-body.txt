# Is the other player's sword a physical thing in this game? (Physics queue P1, VR_TODO.md.)
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-sword-body
#
# The bot's copy holds a drawn iron sword in the pose the bot replays (the player's own stream, as in
# live-weapon-grip), and the bot goes on sending the last of that pose afterwards ("holdpose"): a stream without a
# pose hands the copy back to its animation, and its sword's body leaves the world with it. A plate is dropped just
# above the centre of mass of the sword's body. With nothing physical there it falls through to the ground; with the
# sword a physical body, it hits the blade -- Havok says so (VRWeaponBody: "... touches a body on layer 4"). It does
# not stay there: a plate does not balance on a blade's edge, and in the rig the sword is held low (no controllers,
# so the recorded arms hang; 2026-10-06).
# The client also logs what the Havok world holds for the copy ("PhysicsProbe: actor ...") and which collision
# layers collide with which, once per session.
log live sword body
waitfor connected 45
# Daylight, for a screenshot that shows something (the test game's clock; her saves are put back afterwards).
log DO console set gamehour to 13
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
wait 3

# The bot's own character holds the sword (a copy draws only what it has equipped) and says it is drawn ("draw on").
# The server keeps that and sends it with the character the next time it is spawned for someone, so the bot leaves
# range and comes back: the copy is spawned again "with its weapon drawn", and the client draws it
# (SetWeaponDrawnEx, Actor::SetWeaponDrawn -- which on VR called the wrong virtual until 2026-10-06: "set drawn; the
# game now says sheathed", both passes).
additem 12EB7
equip 12EB7
wait 1
draw on
wait 1
teleport away
wait 6
teleport back
wait 8
log CHECK logged Player copy \w+ came with its weapon drawn
log CHECK logged Weapon of player copy \w+ set drawn \(first pass\); the game went from sheathed to (wantToDraw|drawing|drawn)

# The player draws too, so the pose the bot replays has a sword held (VRPose::HasWeapons) and the copy's is placed in it.
log DO console player.additem 12EB7 1
log DO console player.equipitem 12EB7
wait 2
log DO papyrus Actor DrawWeapon 14
wait 4

# In view of the headset (see live-hands), or the copy is not posed at all.
log DO papyrus ObjectReference MoveTo 14 copy f:50 f:0 f:0 b:false
wait 3
log -- recording the player's stream for 12 s
capture other 12
wait 1
handtargets -15 15 70 15 20 80
holdpose on
replay me 3
wait 3

log CHECK logged PhysicsProbe: actor \w+ --
log CHECK logged PhysicsProbe: actor \w+ right weapon drawn at
# The sword's own body in the world (VRWeaponBody), where Havok keeps it: read back right where it was put (the motion
# state offset is right), then within a few units of the drawn sword frame after frame (the drive works).
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon .* is a body in the world now: layer 8
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon .* placed, it reads [0-2]\.\d units from where it was put
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon body [0-9]\.\d units from where the weapon is drawn
log DO drop 31941 onto copy weapon body 12
wait 3
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ (left|right) weapon touches a body on layer 4
log SHOT sword of copy

# The copy leaves range and its sword's body leaves the world with it.
holdpose off
teleport away
wait 4
log CHECK logged VRWeaponBody: actor \w+ right weapon body out of the world
teleport back
wait 8
log CHECK game alive
wait 3
log done
stop
