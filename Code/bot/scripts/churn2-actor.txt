# The joining and leaving half. Four round trips, each with a few seconds in the world.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair churn2
#
# The dwell matters. Written without it this bot was in the world for about 150 milliseconds per round, which is
# nothing a player does and too short for the other side to see at all -- the test then failed on the watcher never
# having caught a glimpse, which says nothing about removal.

log churn actor
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership
record churn actor

waitfor players >= 1 30
expect known me

log settling so the other side has a clear look before anything churns
wait 10

log round 1
disconnect
wait 2
reconnect
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
wait 3

log round 2
disconnect
wait 1
reconnect
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
wait 3

log round 3
disconnect
wait 3
reconnect
waitfor connected 45
expect known me
wait 3

log round 4, the quickest turnaround the server will take
disconnect
wait 1
reconnect
waitfor connected 45
expect known me

# 77 is a marker, not a health test: "present" looks the same to the watcher in the middle of the churn as it does
# at the end, so the two halves need something to agree on. This is the agreed thing, and it lets the watcher stop
# waiting the instant the rounds are over.
health 77
wait 10
expect connected

record off
report bot-churn2-actor.txt
log done
stop
