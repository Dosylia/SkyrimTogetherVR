# A player's follower belongs to that player's game, whoever leads the party (Emma, 2026-10-07). The leading half.
#
#   powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Tools\VR\run-bot-tests.ps1 -Pair follower
#
# This side joins first and keeps the party's leadership ("party lead"), and brings Lydia (A2C94, a real reference,
# not a temporary actor). Its partner, a plain member, asks for her as its own follower and must get her; then this
# side asks for her as a leader's game asks for what it loads, and must not get her back.
log follower watcher
party lead
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn ownership party
waitfor known other 40
ownership accept
log bringing Lydia, the other side's follower to be
npc A2C94
waitfor owned == 1 30
expect owned == 1
log waiting for the other side to ask for her as its follower
waitfor owned == 0 30
expect owned == 0
log asking for her back as the party leader: refused while she is the other player's follower
claim ref:A2C94
wait 4
expect owned == 0
wait 6
log done
stop
