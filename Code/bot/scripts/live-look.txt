# Pictures of the other player's copy, taken by the game itself: the one kind of check a number cannot do.
#
#   python Tools\VR\live-check.py live-look
#
# `log SHOT <name> of copy` puts a free camera in front of the game's copy of this bot and takes a screenshot (saved
# under build\...\logs\shots). Nothing judges the pictures; they are there to be looked at, and they answer "is
# the body drawn" in a way no actor value ever did -- every reading of an invisible copy on 2026-09-20 and after
# said it was fine. A second picture shows the player from the same distance, for comparison.
log live look
waitfor connected 45
collect spawn
expect known me
waitfor known other 40
health 100
wait 8
log CHECK copy exists
log CHECK copy 3d
wait 2

log -- a picture of the copy, and one of the player
log SHOT copy of copy
wait 4
log SHOT player of player
wait 4

log done
stop
